Garden furniture as feature types: table, chair, bench, sun lounger and parasol. Each is drawn
through its taped corners like every other feature, in warm wood on the map and on its own DXF
layer (`FEATURE-TABLE`, …). Choosing one closes the outline by default. The type picker groups
"Built" and "Furniture", with readable names, and the name field suggests one per type.
