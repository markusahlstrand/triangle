Every view is a URL. `/gardens/:id/map`, a list tab as its collection (`/gardens/:id/measurements`),
a sheet as its resource (`/gardens/:id/points/:pointId`, `/measurements/new?from=…`, `?on=map` when it
opened over the map) and `/members`. Links can be shared and bookmarked, reload keeps the view,
and the back button closes a sheet.
