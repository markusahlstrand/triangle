⌘K / Ctrl-K opens the survey assistant anywhere in a garden, and it now computes instead of
guessing. Its tools: impossible measurements (every taped triangle that cannot close, ranked by
how many triangles a pull breaks, never blaming a verified pull), the range a distance can take,
what-if re-solves (drop or re-read pulls, force a point onto its mirror side — nothing is saved)
and a typo search (a zero too many or too few, two digits swapped). The transcript shows each
analysis it ran before its answer.
