Triangle
1a · v1
A field tool for surveying a private garden with a 20 m tape. Points are triangulated from pairwise distances; features and plants are built on those points. Screens below are static, at phone (375) and desktop widths. The map on Map view pans and zooms — drag it, scroll to zoom.

F · Constraints — new, from your note
Right angles and parallels as solver input
A constraint is another equation the solver gets for free. A right angle at a corner is worth roughly one distance; a parallel pair fixes the orientation of a whole run. On a rectangular terrace, two edge lengths plus a right angle replace the diagonal — one less tape pull per corner, and the diagonal is usually the awkward one.
90° at a point, between two named neighbours
≫parallel — two segments carry matching chevrons
=equal length, for repeated bays or steps
—colinear, for points along one wall face
Constraints are assumptions, not measurements, so they carry their own residual. If the tape later disagrees with an assumed right angle, the constraint is what goes amber — the app should never quietly bend real numbers to fit one.
